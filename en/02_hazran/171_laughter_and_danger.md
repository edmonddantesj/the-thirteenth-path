# Episode 171. The Children Who Laughed First

That day the section,
for longer than the old layer usually allowed, stayed at peace.

It wasn't a brief lull.
For a real while,
it felt like a time when no one had to keep their voice down.

The children were still careful at first.

Even after they had seen the one android with a face,
that hadn't made the fear go away all at once.

But it had clearly changed something.

From the moment eyes and a mouth appeared on a smooth face,
it seemed to have become a little harder, among the children, to call them only "those things."

Maybe that was why, that day,
when one went close,
another found the nerve too,
and when one laughed,
the child beside them finally laughed along.

Han Jiwoo watched from a distance,
and in the end, once again, stayed close.

Partly the children were less afraid because she was there,
and partly she herself didn't want to take her eyes off the scene.

One child said it like a declaration.

"This time I'm drawing this one's face."

Another child put a hand up at once.

"Me, this one."

"Then me, over here."

There was no reason or logic in any of it.
But strangely,
it felt natural, as if who would go to whom had already been settled.

The children stood in front of the androids one by one.

One child drew the eyes very big,
one drew the corners of the eyes lifted all the way up into a smiling face,
one drew the mouth scrunched up and then laughed at it.

"Because this one's kind of grumpy anyway."

At that,
the child beside them burst into giggles.

Another child drew the mouth too crooked,
rubbed it once with a palm to blur it,
and drew it again, rounder.

"This one looks silly."

At that, even from the adults, a very small laugh slipped out.

Luhai covered his mouth with his hand but laughed in the end,
and Sion, too, noticed a beat late that he was laughing.

Seorin didn't stop the laughter.
Sern, record slate in hand, stopped writing for a long while, just this once,
and Ater stood leaning against the wall and didn't look away.

Even the old machines and the people from Hazran
hadn't pulled all the way back out this time.

They didn't come very close,
but at least they didn't look away.

That seemed like the biggest change in the scene.

Feeling that something frightening was frightening,
and still staying in the same space to the end, watching.

That was harder than it sounded.

Each time the children drew a face in with their own hands,
the lines rose one by one, after a small delay, as light from inside the surface.

Round eyes.
Narrow eyes.
A short mouth.
A long, wide grinning mouth.
A crooked mouth.

Every android's face got a different expression.

And they too
seemed to feel the change.

One slowly turned its head to the side, toward a metal panel where its face was reflected,
one looked at the face of the android beside it and then brought its gaze back to its own,
and another, when a child traced its eyes over once more with a finger,
leaned very slightly that way.

All of those movements were clumsy.

They were nothing like the exact beat of carrying out a command.
If anything, they were closer to movements that kept checking a newly made self even while finding it strange,
a little awkward and a little slow.

The children kept laughing as they watched.

"Look, it's looking at itself."
"Whoa, it really is."
"Smiling suits this one."
"Mine's better than that."

While the laughter filled the room,
it struck Sion that he would probably remember this scene for a long time.

Probably, even after all of it was over,
these strange faces from today would be what came to mind first.

There were no names yet.
But there were faces.
And having a face also meant
it was getting hard now to see them as something entirely empty.

Han Jiwoo watched and breathed very slowly.

She was still afraid.
She still didn't want to pin down what it meant too quickly.

But at least,
that the responses the androids were showing right now
couldn't be explained away by simple system error or leftover command resonance alone
was getting harder and harder to deny.

One child spoke in front of the android they had given a smiling mouth with their own hand.

"Do you like it?"

Of course there was no answer.

But a few seconds later,
both ends of the mouth line brightened, very faintly.

The child who saw it went round-eyed,
turned around and shouted,

"It's smiling!"

At that the other children burst into giggles all over again.

That laughter was so unfamiliar inside the old layer
that it sounded all the more precious, and all the more painful.

An old machine was watching the scene quietly from a distance.
Its optical eye was wavering, very faintly.

That reaction wasn't simple wariness.
It looked closer to surprise and hesitation,
and perhaps to the stillness of a moment when something lost a very long time ago appears before you again.

Ater said, very low,

"They each respond differently."

Sern answered quietly.

"Yes."
"They were given the same thing, a face,
yet they do not look at it in the same way."

Luhai muttered, still looking as if he could hardly believe it.

"So really…
each one's taking it in differently."

No one could say that for certain.
But no one could dismiss it as wrong as easily as before, either.

A woman from Hazran stood behind the children,
watching with slightly wet eyes, and said very softly,

"So you could laugh like that."

Who the words were meant for,
the children didn't know, and the adults didn't ask.

Most likely they were meant for the children
and, at the same time, for herself.

That was when it happened.

Han Jiwoo looked up first.
More precisely,
she was the first to feel something shift.

The air cooled, just slightly.
Before any sound had arrived,
an unfamiliar tension was seeping in first from somewhere in the space.

Two of the old machines stopped almost at the same moment.
One outside hand turned its eyes first, not toward the children who had been drawing faces,
but toward the entrance of the gray route.

Only when he saw that did Sion feel the laughter, very slowly, dying away.

Nothing had happened yet.
But everyone,
at the same moment, felt that something was about to come in wrong.

Seorin said, short,

"What is it?"

The answer came from somewhere else first.

A man who had been among the Hazran people,
who had definitely been right there until a moment ago, was nowhere to be seen.

Someone said, low,

"Wait."
"Where'd he go?"

The moment the words fell,
Han Jiwoo's face stiffened, very faintly.

He was the man who had been rescued and brought in a few days ago.
He'd been injured,
said little,
a man who always kept close to the wall.

So quiet
that, if anything, his presence had faded.

And for exactly that reason,
the fact that he was gone reached everyone several beats late.

Sion felt something go cold somewhere behind his back.

At that moment,
one of the androids that had just been given a smiling mouth turned its head first.

Not toward the children,
but toward the darkness past the gray route, where no sound could be heard yet.

Then two other androids moved their eyes in nearly the same direction.

This time it was too clear
for anyone to call it chance.

One step at a time,
very slowly,
they began to move themselves, not in front of the children, but between the children and that darkness.

There was no command.
No one told them to.

But the movement, unmistakably,
was preparation.

Sion watched, holding his breath.

The danger couldn't be seen yet.
But the first to sense it here,
it seemed, might well have been them.

Beings who had gained faces,
been given laughter,
and begun to accept strange expressions as their own
were now starting to stand in front of the children by their own will.

At that moment,
the air in the old layer changed completely, once again.
