# Episode 181. More Than Surviving

The time they were given after the fighting broke off was not long.

No one could easily call it rest.
It was closer to a gap just wide enough to catch their breath before Serakion came again,
a very short reprieve in which to lay the wounded down,
block the collapsed routes for the time being,
and sort out the next move.

But even inside that short time,
the air in the old layer was clearly changing, little by little.

The last android was moved farther inside.

Not right at the front line,
but to a quiet section a little apart
from where the children and the wounded were gathered.

Han Jiwoo stayed by his side.

At first she had to start over from sorting what she could touch from what she couldn't.

Some of the cracked outer casing she could carefully clear away,
and the misaligned axis she could set back, just a little.
But past a certain point,
she was no longer sure she could even call this simple repair.

At places where once she would not have hesitated,
her hands now stopped.

This was not just a broken machine.
Until a moment ago he had been a being who protected others by his own will,
who was shaken by a child's words,
who checked behind him to the very end.

So at some point Han Jiwoo realized
that before touching any part,
she was paying more attention to moving in a way that would not startle him.

"Hold on,"
she said, very low.
"This might hurt a lit…"

The words broke off there.

Because she herself had been the first to notice
that the word "hurt" had slipped carelessly out of her own mouth.

A child nearby seemed to have heard it too,
and looked at the last android with a face about to cry.

Then, very carefully,
with a much more familiar motion than a little while ago, the child sat down by his side.

"Will it be over soon?"

Han Jiwoo couldn't answer right away.
But the last android's optical eye flickered, very faintly, toward the child.

By now, small movements like that
did not look like chance to anyone there.

One by one, the children gathered near him again.

Of course they were all still careful.
The shock of the battle had not completely worn off,
and they had not yet finished crying for all the faces they had lost.

But strangely,
that seemed to be exactly why they kept going to this last one's side.

Because it was a face that remained.
Because it was a being still here.
And because only by checking again and again
could they hold on to the feeling that not everything had completely vanished.

One child, with the dust on their hand,
very carefully drew the faded short mouth line again.
Another started to trace a slightly rounded corner of an eye beside it with a finger,
then quickly pulled back when Han Jiwoo, with a face that could neither smile nor cry, stopped them.

"Leave one for later,"
she said, low.
"We can't touch too much right now."

The children took that with surprising seriousness.

It was an attitude like keeping your voice down when you visit someone sick.
No one had taught them that,
but they were all already treating that being that way.

Watching the scene from a little way off,
Sion felt something somewhere in his chest quietly shake.

What was happening here was not simple recovery.

It was people taking that being deeper inside their community.
And at the same time,
that being seemed to be taking in, little by little,
the words and touches and silences set down among the people.

The old machines didn't come close,
but they didn't stand at their old distance either.

One kept watching the perimeter routes from afar,
and another, when Han Jiwoo looked for a piece of a part she needed, silently brought it and set it down.

All of it moved so naturally
that Sion was suddenly saddened by the fact that this sight could not last much longer.

Serakion would surely come again.
And next time it would come far more precisely than this time,
far more cruelly.

Ater came back and said, low,

"The flow outside has gone wrong."
"There is not much time."

At that, the air cooled a little again.

It felt like something they had all known
settling down once more as reality.

Seorin looked at the last android for a moment.
Then she looked at the children.
She looked at Han Jiwoo.

She didn't say anything right away.
But within that silence,
Sion could tell the calculation had already started again.

This second battle would follow the bones of the last plan.
Dividing the layers,
dividing the roles,
the way each of them broke down the path they were given would itself stay the same.

But now one variable had changed completely.

On that side there were still command-driven androids.
And on this side,
there was an android with a will.

That one fact
was forcing the whole meaning of the next battle to be rewritten.

A child whispered to the last android, very small,

"I hope you don't get hurt this time."

At that,
the last android's optical eye flickered once more, very faintly.

And Sion
felt that the moment was so warm it hurt all the more.

Because he felt, dimly, that he already knew
that scenes exactly like this one
were why they would not be able to simply leave that being here.

Staying beside the children would not be peace.
If Serakion came again,
danger would follow that being and break into this place first of all.

Because they wanted to be together,
the moment would come when they had to leave together instead.

No one had said it aloud yet.
But in the middle of that short rest,
that unavoidable future was already, very quietly, showing its shape.
