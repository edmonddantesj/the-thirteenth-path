# Episode 187. Judgment Split in Two

The man was still reaching out.

"Please…"
"I can't go alone…"

His fingers shook.
He pushed himself along the floor on his knees once more and came forward.

Han Jiwoo took a step out.
Before her second step could land,
Seorin's hand caught her arm.

She didn't grip hard.
She didn't let go either.

"Split it in two."

Seorin said it low.

Han Jiwoo looked at Seorin.
Seorin was looking at the man.

"Sion, cut the right."
"Ater, watch above."
"Sern, the second the rear lane closes, say so."
"Jiwoo, you watch him and the man both."

With each order, one person moved.
Sion turned to the right.
Ater's head went up.
Sern opened the record panel again.
Seorin's hand came off Han Jiwoo's arm.
Han Jiwoo did not take back the step she had taken.
From there she looked from the man to the android and back.

Sern spoke, watching the rear.

"Two rear rows. They are not moving yet."

"Tell me when they move."

Seorin answered.

Sion threw himself into the gap on the right.

The corridor behind the man was smooth.
The gray panels,
the low guide lights,
all of it straight.

Up close,
there was one more line running between the wall and the floor.
Thinner than the other seams.
Straighter than the other seams.

Sion took his blade in both hands.
He drove the tip into the seam and put his weight on it.
His palm slipped.
He took hold again.

Sion scraped the gap briefly with the tip of his blade.
A low locking sound rang out from inside at once.

"That's it!"

He kept the shout low.

"The path itself folds!"

Before he had finished,
a piece of metal meshed somewhere above.

Ater spoke at once.

"It is closing from above!"
"Three seconds!"

The two Hazran men raised their wall panels over their heads.
Their arms shook.

"Two!"

Sern was watching the rear.

"Two rear rows are moving."
"If we pull the man out, the main line opens."

Sern's finger touched two spots on the record panel.
Where the man stood,
and the main line behind them.
His finger went back and forth once between the two.

The last android moved.

He did not abandon the front.
Nor did he rush straight at the man.
Turning his body very briefly,
he took a place as wide as his own shoulders between the man and the gap behind him.
He stopped there.
His optical eye looked up once.
At the gap once.
At the man once.

From where she had stepped back, Han Jiwoo spoke almost like a breath.

"Left, now."
"Just one space."

He moved half a step to the left.
A panel falling from above
grazed past behind his shoulder and drove into the floor.
Where it drove in was where he had been standing.

Han Jiwoo's mouth fell open.
No sound came.
She closed it.

Sion gritted his teeth and tore the side-line junction open further.
Inside the wall, the plate that had been about to fold stopped halfway.
The stopped plate made a sound.

One of the old machines seized two Serakion units pushing in from the main line
and drove them into the opposite wall.
It laid its own body over the two it had driven in and pressed down.
The pressed two moved beneath it.
The machine's chest plate rang.
The machine beside it came and pressed down too.
Under the weight of two, they stopped.

"One!"

Ater's voice dropped from above.

In the middle of it all, the man was crying.

"Save me…"
"Please, please…"

His face was bowed nearly to the floor.
His outstretched hand was still outstretched.

Han Jiwoo looked at that hand.
She looked at the last android's back.
She looked at that hand again.

She spoke very low.

"We can't abandon him either."

The last android's optical eye flickered, very briefly.
It turned toward the man.
Then it turned toward the gap.
His body stayed between the two.

"Now!"

Seorin raised her voice.

Sion sliced clean through one of the wall's folding axes.
The plate that had been half-folding stopped where it was.
Ater dropped the upper drop-plate first.
The plate, falling two beats before it should have,
struck floor where no one stood.
Sern set two old machines in front of the main-line gap that had been about to open behind them.
The two put their shoulders together, and the gap was covered.

The last android,
just before the very last space closed,
twisted his body and slipped in front of the man.

He didn't run in a straight line.
He shoved the folding angle with his shoulder and wrenched it aside.
It was the twisted shoulder.
His shoulder made a sound.
So that the man's body would not be pulled in,
he pushed the man's back with his other arm.
The man rolled outward.

The sound of metal closing burst out right behind them.

The closed wall was a palm's width from his back.

Sion let his breath out.
The hand holding his blade was shaking.
He shifted his grip on the blade.

The man lay collapsed on the floor on the outer side, doing nothing but gasping.
His face was against the floor.
The hand he had reached out with lay open on the floor.

Han Jiwoo went to him.

She knelt, took the man by the shoulder and turned him over.
He was breathing.
His eyes were open, but they did not look at her.
She took hold of his wrist.
His pulse was fast.
She did not let go.

The man's mouth moved.

"…It was open."

Han Jiwoo bent her head to listen.

"The door… because it was open…"

"I know."

Han Jiwoo said.

"Stop talking."

The man closed his mouth.
His eyes turned toward the closed wall.
The wall was smooth.
Where the gap had been, there was nothing.
The man looked at that wall and shook.
Still holding his wrist, Han Jiwoo blocked his view with her own body.
So he could not see the wall.

Seorin stood over the two of them.
She looked down at the man.
Han Jiwoo looked up.

"Send him back."

Seorin said.

"Leave him here and they'll use him again."

Han Jiwoo nodded.
The man's face said he had not heard.

Luhai came back from the right axis.
He crouched in front of the man.
He looked at the man's face.
He set his iron bar down on the floor.

"Can you walk?"

The man did not answer.
Luhai picked up the iron bar and stood.
He looked at the two Hazran men.

"Back. To the gap."

The two of them nodded.

The last android stood with his back to the closed wall.
Between the man and the wall.
The twisted shoulder sat lower than before.
He did not look at that shoulder.
He was looking at the man's wrist in Han Jiwoo's hand.

Seorin spoke low.

"We keep going."
"It'll get trickier from here."

Sern spoke, looking to the rear.

"Two rear rows are pulling back."

Seorin looked that way.

"They're not pulling back."
"They're regrouping."

Sern wrote one line on the record panel.
Ater came down from above.
Coming down, he did not brush the dust off his shoulder.

"Every upper plate has dropped."
"They can no longer come that way."

"They'll come another way."

Seorin answered.

"They will."

Ater nodded.
After nodding, he looked at his own ankle.
Where the arm of a falling unit had grazed it earlier, metal had left a scrape.
It wasn't bleeding.
He ran his hand over the scrape once and stood up.

Sion rubbed the palm that had held the blade on his trousers.
The sweat didn't come off.
He did not sheathe the blade; he kept it in hand.

Sern added more.

"Two main-line rows have pulled back five steps."

The two old machines stepped back from the gap.
The two units they had driven in were still pinned to the wall.
The gap did not open.

Han Jiwoo looked at the last android's shoulder.
One knot had come undone.

"One knot's come loose."

She said.
She let go of the man's wrist and stood.
She put her hand on the android's shoulder.
She pulled the loose cloth and tied it again.
While she tied it, the android's optical eye watched her hands.
Once it was tied, she tugged it.
It held.

Han Jiwoo stood beside the last android.
She looked at his lowered shoulder.
She put her hand on the knot.
It touched.

Briefly.

Then she lowered her hand and looked ahead.

The man could not get himself up off the floor.
The two Hazran men hooked his arms on either side.

The last android took his back off the closed wall.
He took one step out.
His right leg dragged once.
He set that leg right and took the second step.
Han Jiwoo fell in half a step behind.
She was looking at the dust on his back.

Seorin walked first.
