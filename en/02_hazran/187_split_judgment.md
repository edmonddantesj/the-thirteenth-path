# Episode 187. Judgment Split in Two

The man was still reaching out.

"Please… I can't go alone…"

Fingers trembling, the man pushed himself along the floor on his knees once more and came forward.

Han Jiwoo took a step out. Before her second step could land, Seorin's hand caught her arm.

She didn't grip hard. But she didn't let go either.

"Split it in two."

Seorin spoke low.

Han Jiwoo looked at Seorin, but Seorin was looking at the man.

"Sion, cut off the right. Ater, watch above. Sern, the moment the rear route closes, say so. Jiwoo, you watch both—that one and the man."

With each order, one person moved. Sion turned to the right, Ater's head went up, and Sern opened the record panel again. Seorin's hand came off Han Jiwoo's arm. Without taking back the step she had taken, Han Jiwoo looked from the man to the android and back from where she stood.

Sern spoke, watching the rear.

"Two rear rows. They are not moving yet."

"Tell me when they move."

Seorin answered.

Sion threw himself into the gap on the right.

The corridor behind the man was smooth, and the gray panels and the low guide light were all straight.

Up close, he could see one more line set in between wall and floor. It was thinner than the other seams, and straighter than the other seams.

Sion took his blade in both hands. He drove the tip into the seam and put his weight on it.
His palm slipped. He took hold again.

When he scraped the gap briefly with the tip of the blade, a low locking sound rang out from inside at once.

"That's it!"

He kept the shout low.

"The path itself folds!"

Before he had finished, a piece of metal meshed somewhere above.

Ater spoke at once.

"It is closing from above! Three seconds!"

The two Hazran men raised their wall panels over their heads, their arms shaking.

"Two!"

Sern was watching the rear.

"Two rear rows are moving. If we recover the man, the main line opens."

Sern's finger touched two spots on the record panel: where the man stood, and the main line behind them. His finger went back and forth once between the two.

The last android moved.

He did not abandon the front, but nor did he rush straight at the man. Turning his body very briefly, he took a space the width of his shoulders between the man and the gap behind him, and stopped there.
His optical eye looked up once. At the gap once. At the man once.

From where she had stepped out, Han Jiwoo spoke almost like a breath.

"Now, left. Just one space."

The moment he shifted half a step left, a panel falling from above grazed past behind his shoulder and drove into the floor. Where it drove in was where he had been standing.

Han Jiwoo's mouth opened, but no sound came, and it closed again.

Sion gritted his teeth and tore the side-line junction open further. Inside the wall, a plate that had been about to fold stopped halfway, with a noise.

One of the old machines seized two Serakion units pushing in from the main line and drove them into the opposite wall. It laid its own body over the two it had driven in and pressed down.
The pressed two moved beneath it. The machine's chest plate rang.
The machine beside it came and pressed down too. Under the weight of two, they stopped.

"One!"

Ater's voice dropped from above.

In the middle of it all, the man was crying.

"Save me… please, please…"

Though his head was bowed so low his face touched the floor, his outstretched hand was still outstretched.

Han Jiwoo looked at that hand, then at the last android's back, then at that hand again.

She spoke very low.

"We can't abandon him either."

The last android's optical eye flickered, very briefly. It turned toward the man, then toward the gap. His body stayed between the two.

"Now!"

Seorin raised her voice.

Sion sliced clean through one of the wall's folding axes. The plate that had been half-folding stopped where it was.

Ater dropped the upper drop-plate first. Falling two beats before it should have, the plate struck floor where no one stood. Sern set the two old machines in front of the main-line gap that had been about to open behind them, and when the two put their shoulders together, the gap was covered.

The last android, just before the very last space closed, twisted his body and slipped in front of the man.

He didn't run in a straight line.
He shoved the folding angle with his shoulder and wrenched it aside. It was the twisted shoulder.
His shoulder made a sound.
So that the man's body would not be pulled in, he pushed the man's back with his other arm.
The man rolled outward.

The sound of metal closing burst out right behind them.

The closed wall was a palm's width from his back.

Even as Sion let out his breath, the hand holding his blade was shaking, so he shifted his grip on the blade.

The man lay collapsed on the floor on the outer side, doing nothing but gasping. His face was against the floor, and the hand he had reached out lay open on it.

Han Jiwoo went to him.

She knelt, took the man by the shoulder and turned him over; he was breathing. His eyes were open, but they did not look at her. She took hold of his wrist. His pulse was fast. She did not let go.

The man's mouth moved.

"…It was open."

Han Jiwoo bent her head to listen.

"The door… because it was open…"

"I know. Stop talking."

The man closed his mouth, and his eyes turned toward the closed wall. The wall was smooth; where the gap had been, there was nothing. When the man began to shake, looking at that wall, Han Jiwoo, still holding his wrist, blocked his view with her own body. So he could not see the wall.

Seorin stood over the two of them and looked down at the man, and Han Jiwoo looked up.

"Send him back. Leave him here and they'll use him again."

Han Jiwoo nodded, but the man's face said he had not heard.

Luhai came back from the right axis and crouched in front of the man. Crouching there, he looked at the man's face for a while, then set his iron bar down on the floor.

"Can you walk?"

When the man didn't answer, Luhai picked up the iron bar, stood, and looked at the two Hazran men.

"Back. To the gap."

The two of them nodded.

The last android stood with his back to the closed wall, between the man and the wall. The twisted shoulder hung lower than before, but he did not look at that shoulder. He was looking at the man's wrist in Han Jiwoo's hand.

Seorin spoke low.

"We keep going. They'll get craftier now."

Sern spoke, looking to the rear.

"Two rear rows are pulling back."

Seorin looked that way.

"They're not pulling back. They're rearranging."

While Sern wrote one line on the record panel, Ater came down from above. Coming down, he did not brush the dust off his shoulder.

"The upper plates have all fallen. They cannot come that way anymore."

"They'll come another way."

Seorin answered.

"They will."

Ater nodded, then looked at his own ankle. Where the arm of a falling unit had grazed him earlier, metal had left a scrape, but it wasn't bleeding. He ran his hand over the scrape once and stood up.

Sion rubbed the palm that had held his blade on his trousers, but the sweat didn't come off. He did not put the blade away; he kept it in his hand.

Sern added more.

"Two main-line rows have pulled back five steps."

The two old machines stepped back from the gap. The two units they had driven in stayed pinned to the wall, and the gap did not open.

When Han Jiwoo looked at the last android's shoulder, one knot had come undone.

"One knot's come loose."

She said that, then let go of the man's wrist and stood.

She put her hand on the android's shoulder, pulled the loose cloth and tied it again. While she tied it, the android's optical eye watched her hands. When she had tied it she gave it a tug, and it held.

She stood beside the last android and looked at his lowered shoulder. She put her hand on the knot. It touched.

Briefly.

Then she lowered her hand and looked ahead.

The man could not get himself up off the floor. The two Hazran men hooked his arms on either side.

The last android took his back off the closed wall and stepped out once. When his right leg dragged, he set that leg right and took the second step. Han Jiwoo kept close half a step behind, looking at the dust on his back.

Seorin walked first.
