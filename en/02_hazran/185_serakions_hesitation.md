# Episode 185. Serakion's First Hesitation

The change in the circulation hum inside Serakion
reached the last android first.

His optical eye flickered, very briefly,
and beside him, holding her breath, Han Jiwoo watched that flicker.
The old machine took its hand off the wall.

"Is it here?"

Han Jiwoo asked.

The machine nodded.
It did not speak.
The two Hazran men's wall panels went up.
The arms of the man without a younger brother shook.
He did not lower his.

Right after that,
from the far end of the gray route ahead
came a run of metal sounds.
It was the same sound they had heard in the first battle.
The spacing was different.

"The spacing's different."

Sion said.

"Longer."

"Shorter."

Sern said.

"It has become shorter. More of them are coming."

Sion took his blade in both hands.

Serakion's android legion did not come flooding in at once.
Seorin raised her hand.
Every axis stopped.
Left.
Right.
Above.
Center.
Breathing.
The sound of wall panels going up.
Nothing else.

The first line stepped into the mouth of the route
and stopped for one beat.
Not enough to see.
Their footsteps slipped out of step once.
The feet of the front row and the feet of the row behind landed apart.

Sion heard it.
Seorin heard it too.
Sern's hand stopped above the record panel.

"They landed apart."

Sion said.

"Last time it was one step."

"I heard."

Seorin said.

"Why."

"Because of him."

Sern said.

"Two optical eyes in the front row saw him. Having seen him, their feet came late."

"Now."

Seorin said it low.

With that one word,
each axis moved.

The team that had gone in by the left route
made a short cut in the surface panel at the point set in advance.
Behind the panel, the light of the circulation line slowed once.
The right-hand recovery axis pried two Serakion units off the main line to the side.
As the two it had pried loose were pushed toward the wall,
the line behind them split in two.
The ones covering the upper level twisted a guide rail before the drop line could form.
A unit that dropped where the rail was twisted
landed on floor two steps off from where it was meant to land.
Ater looked down at that unit.
Two steps.
The unit got up and went back to its place.
Two steps.
On foot.
For those two steps the next unit could not drop.
It waited on the rail.
Ater twisted the rail once more.

The first line of the Serakion legion pushed into view.

Faceless surfaces.
Unwavering speed.
The spaces between their shoulders were all the same.
The corridor lights fell on those surfaces exactly alike.
Han Jiwoo looked at those surfaces.
There were no lines.
Not one.
She looked at the face beside her.
Faint lines.
She looked ahead again.

The optical eyes of the two units at the very front, almost at once,
turned toward the last android.

And again,
a pause came.
Han Jiwoo counted the pause.
One.
Two.

The two stood with their feet on the floor, and the next step did not lift.
The line behind bumped their backs and drew off.

Seorin was watching.

"Push."

"Where to?"

Luhai shouted.
From the right.

"Where they stopped. The stopped ones can't take it."

The moment the word fell,
an old machine on the side line knocked an enemy unit off its center first.
The fallen unit landed across the feet of the unit beside it.
Ater wrenched aside the entry angle of the next line coming up behind.
He kicked a wall panel and cut the width of the route in half.
Two tried to come through the narrowed width at once, and their shoulders struck.

The last android did not go out head-on.

"Go."

Han Jiwoo said.

"Only the stopped ones."

From the same axis as Han Jiwoo,
the android turned toward the two units that had stopped at the very front.

One step.
A short turn.
Stop.

He stood in that spot.
The two units' optical eyes turned to follow him.
Their bodies did not turn.
Optical eyes and bodies facing different ways, they stopped a beat longer.
Sion saw it.
Eyes and body apart.
A stance that would put a person on the floor.
They did not fall.
They stood.
Wrong.

He went into that one beat.
He buckled the front unit's knee
and shoved the buckled unit into the one behind.
The two went down in a heap.

His shoulder axis made a sound.
He did not reach for the shoulder.
He stepped back once, to his original place.
Han Jiwoo looked at that shoulder.
Three knots.
Holding.

"You okay?"

He looked ahead.
She took that as an answer.
She gripped the edge of the wall panel again.

Watching it, Sern spoke almost under his breath.

"Their reactions are splitting."

"What's splitting?"

Seorin asked.

"The ones that saw him and the ones that did not. The ones that saw him are late."

"By how much?"

"One beat. Always one beat."

"One beat's enough."

The Serakion legion could not come in like a single wave.
Some turned their optical eyes toward the last android,
some went only for the target point along the existing command line.
The feet of those that turned and those that did not landed apart.
One line walked in two beats.

"Two beats."

Sern said.

"One line is walking as two. It is the first time."

"And if it's two?"

Seorin asked.

"A space opens. Between front and back."

"Go into the space."

Seorin said.

Through that gap
the left team cut one more panel.
The right team pried off one more unit.

Kael shouted from the left.

"Two panels left!"

"Save them!"

Seorin shouted back.

"Use them on the next line!"

Kael stopped his hands.
The old machine took its hand off the panel.
The light of the circulation line stayed slow.

Han Jiwoo held the same axis as the last android.

Not right beside him.
Behind the cover she had pulled back to once.
One hand on the edge of a wall panel,
she looked from him to the enemy units and back.
Whenever it was needed, she spoke short and low.

"Left."

He turned left.

"Don't stop now."

He did not stop.

"One more coming."

He took it.

There was no gap between word and movement.
Han Jiwoo's hand gripped the edge of the wall panel.
Her fingertips went white.
The man without a younger brother looked at that hand.
He looked at his own hand.
The hand gripping the wall panel.
White.
He gripped harder.

One of the Serakion legion suddenly broke off the main line.

Not toward the last android.
Toward the safe axis at the rear, where the children were.
Only that unit's footsteps were different.
Fast.

The last android's optical eye turned that way.
His body started to turn.

The right-hand recovery axis got there first.
Luhai's team cut the unit off from the side.
The iron bar went into its knee joint.
The unit hit the wall and stopped.

"Nothing gets to the kids!"

Luhai shouted.

"This is as far as it goes!"

The unit slid down the wall.
Luhai pulled the iron bar out.
He looked at it.
It hadn't bent.
Not yet.
He gripped it again.

The last android's body stopped halfway through the turn.
His optical eye rested one beat on the spot where the unit had fallen.
Han Jiwoo saw that beat.

"The kids have Aka."

Her voice was low.

"Eyes front."

Then he turned back to face the front.

Almost at the same time, Seorin widened the gap in the middle of the main line.

"It's now."

Sion moved.

He went into his own share of the axis
and, just as first planned, brought down a blocked junction.
Metal split.
Through the split, light from the inner circulation line leaked out.

Serakion did not scream.
A low, polite rerouting chime passed once.
Which path had closed and which had opened in its place,
it announced in the same voice.

Before that sound ended, Sion was on the second junction.
He set the tip of the blade in the seam and pressed.
His palm slipped.
A new blade.
A blade his hand didn't know yet.
He took it in both hands.
Pressed.
The second gap opened.
The rerouting chime started again before it had finished.
The second chime ran over the first.
The overlapping words could not be made out.
Two voices.
The same voice.
Covering each other.
Sern heard it.
He could not write it down.
He did not know what it said.

In that moment,
for the first time, in the very middle of the front, the last android
stood face to face with a single Serakion unit.

Two steps lay between them.

The same body.
The same height,
the same shoulder width,
the same optical eyes.

One had no face.
The other still kept the child's faded lines on his face.

The Serakion unit's optical eyes stopped on those lines.

Its feet did not lift.
Sern counted.
One.
Two.
Three.
Han Jiwoo stopped breathing.
The unit's hand rose.
Toward its own face.
Halfway.
It stopped.
It did not come down.

The last android's foot lifted first.

It was one beat.
In that one beat he closed the two steps.
His hand went in between the unit's neck and shoulder.

Until the fallen unit's optical eyes went dark,
he stayed where he was.
Once they were dark, he drew his hand out.
Han Jiwoo watched his back.
The hand drawing out.
Slowly.
He looked at the raised hand of the fallen unit.
On the floor.
A hand stopped halfway up.
He did not lower it for the unit.
He looked.
One beat.
He turned away.

The next line of the Serakion legion stood at the mouth of the route.
Their footsteps were out of step.

"They are out of step."

Sern said.

"It began before they halted."

Ater spoke from above.

"They came having seen it. What became of the front line."

"They see that and still come?"

Luhai asked.

"They come."

Ater said.

"They come having seen it, and their feet are out of step. That is what is different."

Han Jiwoo took her hand off the wall panel.
The edge of the wall panel had marked her palm.
A red line.
She looked at that line.
She put the hand back.
The same place.

Seorin spoke with her eyes ahead.

"Keep going."

Luhai shouted from the right.

"How many lines?"

"I am counting!"

Sern shouted.

"Don't count, just tell me!"

"I do not know!"

Sern shouted.

"For the first time, I do not know!"

Luhai laughed.
Short.
He gripped his iron bar again.
