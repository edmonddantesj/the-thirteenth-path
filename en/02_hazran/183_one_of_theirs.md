# Episode 183. One of Theirs

Preparations to leave moved fast.

While the routes were checked again, Ater sorted the newly blocked paths from the gaps still usable. Three blocked paths, two usable gaps. He touched the five of them on the wall with a finger.

"Which of the two?"

Seorin asked.

"The left. They saw the right one last time."

"They didn't see the left?"

"They will have. Late."

Seorin chose the left.

Sern wrote the damage, the time and the number of people who could move on one page of the record panel again, and the page was more crowded than before. At the line for the head count his hand stopped once, and he wrote only after he had counted again.

"Why'd you stop?"

Seorin asked.

"I was one short."

Sern said.

"When I counted again, it was right. I was wrong."

"Who did you leave out?"

"That android."

Sern said.

"I did not know whether I should put him in the line for people."

Seorin didn't answer; she went over each team's role again, briefly. One word per team, and still no one asked anything.

The Hazran people shared out water and cloth. The cloth had been split into the bloodied and the clean, and there was less of the clean. A woman with a tired face held out a clean one to Han Jiwoo.

"For the shoulder."

The woman said.

What Han Jiwoo took was cloth for the third knot.

Even in the middle of all that, people's heads kept turning back toward the last android.

He was still in the quiet sector. Checking the last fastening device, Han Jiwoo tugged the three knots one by one, and none came loose. An old machine set a newly brought scrap of parts down beside her; it was the third one. When Han Jiwoo picked it up and fitted it inside the shoulder axis, it went in.

"Where did you get it?"

"From their side."

The machine said.

"Where they fell."

Han Jiwoo stopped her hands and looked at the scrap, but she left it where it was.

"It fits. Same body, after all."

Beside him, the children were keeping their voices down. When one child tugged another's sleeve to make them quieter still, the child who was tugged shut their mouth.

One of the children crouched beside the last android and spoke in a very small voice.

"Can't you stay now?"

No one could answer right away.

The android's optical eye turned slowly to the child, then came back to Han Jiwoo. Han Jiwoo couldn't meet it; she looked at the fastening device.

Sern's hand stopped above the record panel, and Ater took his finger off the wall. Seorin watched the child from a distance. The hand of the Hazran woman sharing out water stopped, and a little water spilled on the floor.

For a long while Han Jiwoo couldn't bring herself to speak. Her grip on the fastening device tightened, and one knot cinched a little tighter. Only after she loosened that hand did she turn toward the child.

"If he stays here…"

She spoke very low.

"Next time they'll come to you first."

The child blinked, looked at Han Jiwoo, then at the android. The android's optical eye was on the child.

Han Jiwoo glanced at the last android, then looked back at the child.

"They'll come looking for him. Then it gets dangerous here too."

"Why would they look for him?"

"Because he was one of theirs. They don't know they've lost something of theirs. If they count, they'll know. They'll be counting."

The child looked at the android's face.

The child's eyes went red.

"But…"

The child barely got the word out.

"It's nice when we're together."

A few people in the room turned their heads away. A Hazran woman pressed the back of her hand to her eyes. Luhai, still looking at the ceiling, shifted his grip on the iron bar, and Sern looked at the record panel but didn't write. Luhai brought his eyes down from the ceiling.

"Kids are always the most right. That's why it's harder to give them what they want."

He looked at the ceiling again.

Sion didn't turn his head; he looked at the child's hand. It lay close to the android's knee but didn't touch it. There was still dirt on the fingertips.

The last android's optical eye moved slowly toward the child.

And for a long time, a really long time, he stayed like that. The child held his gaze and didn't move either. Between them, Han Jiwoo held her breath.

The old machine standing at the sector entrance took one step in and stopped. The optical eye of that machine, the one that had drawn the cloth back over the dead, was turned toward the child's dirt-marked fingers. The machine looked at its own hand. There was no dirt on it.

Seorin came over quietly.

She lowered herself a little, as if to meet the child at eye level, and spoke briefly.

"I know it's nice being together. That's why we're taking him."

The child looked at Seorin, but Seorin didn't look away. She watched the child's hand touch the android's knee once and come away, and then she stood.

The children couldn't hold back their tears, but none of them clung or threw themselves on the floor. One child, crying, took another's hand, and that child took the hand of another. The three of them stood holding hands, looking at the android. The fourth child had their arms around a Hazran woman's leg, face still turned toward the android.

Aka stood behind the four of them without a word. One child caught Aka's sleeve, and Aka let it be.

"I'm staying."

She told the children.

"Here."

The children listened without taking their eyes off the android.

The child who had been sticking close to the last android all along touched the faint line of his mouth one last time with a fingertip, as if pressing it on again. The finger stayed on the line. It pressed once more before it lifted.

"Come back."

The last android's optical eye trembled, very faintly. Under the finger, the mouth line brightened once. The child took the finger away. The brightness stayed.

One beat.

Then it went back.

The child looked at their finger and saw that the dirt had rubbed off onto the line, but didn't wipe it away.

A moment later, he moved on his own.

Han Jiwoo held out a hand to support him. Before her hand reached him, he braced a hand against the wall and slowly stood.

He didn't use the hand on the side of the twisted shoulder; he pushed against the wall with the other hand and got himself upright. His knee buckled once. The hand on the wall tightened. His fingers scraped the wall. He stood again. Once he was up, the mouth line the child had drawn sat above the child's eye level, so the child tipped their head back to look at it.

Han Jiwoo's outstretched hand was left in the air. She slowly drew it back and held it in her other hand.

He looked once toward the children. He looked in turn at the three standing hand in hand, the fourth holding on to a leg, and the child who had touched the mouth line, and then he turned toward Sion's group and Seorin.

The turn took three steps. With each step the twisted shoulder swayed and the three knots pulled tight and slackened, but he didn't grab the shoulder.

Sion counted those three steps, then looked at his blade. It was the new one a Hazran man had given him, with no chips in its edge. He gripped it, but it didn't sit right in his hand yet.

Once he had turned, the last android looked at Seorin. Seorin nodded once, but he didn't nod back; he stood where he was, both feet planted on the floor.

"Would be nice if he nodded back."

Luhai said.

"He won't."

Han Jiwoo said.

"Standing is his nod. That's him."

Han Jiwoo closed her eyes and opened them, then murmured, very low.

"I get it."

"Get what?"

Seorin asked.

"He stood up on his own."

Han Jiwoo said.

"Nobody holding him. So he's going."

Seorin nodded.

Han Jiwoo picked up the tools on the floor one by one and put them in her pockets. Two screwdrivers, one pair of pliers, one bundle of leftover cloth. She had given two away earlier, to a child and to the old man, so two places in her pockets were empty, and she put the cloth in those places. After the last one went in, she pressed once on the inner pocket.

She got up and stood beside the android. Close enough to touch with one outstretched hand. She didn't reach out.

The children were watching the two of them. The child who had touched the mouth line raised a hand, not waving it, just holding it up.

The last android's optical eye stayed on that hand.

Seorin turned.

"We're going."

Two old machines moved toward the route first. As they passed the sector entrance, one looked back toward the children, and the other pushed its shoulder. While Ater followed behind them, Sern closed the record panel. Luhai looked once toward the children and started walking.

Two Hazran men, each carrying a wall panel, fell in behind them. The man without a younger brother was one of them. When the woman with the tired face pressed a cloth into his hand, he took it and carried it along with the wall panel.

The last android took a step. His knee held.
It held on the second step too.
On the third, a sound came from his shoulder.
His stride didn't break.

Han Jiwoo walked beside him at the same pace. Half a step behind him, where she could see his back.

The child's hand was still raised.

All the way through the sector entrance and around the corner, Han Jiwoo didn't look back, but the android looked back once. The hand was still raised. Once they rounded the corner the hand was out of sight, and he looked ahead.

From half a step behind, Han Jiwoo watched his back.

Three knots.

She counted. None had come loose.
