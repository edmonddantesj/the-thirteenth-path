# Episode 173. Answered Faces

After the Serakion legion's first wave had broken inward, inside the old layer, sound buried sound.

On top of the sound of metal bending came the sound of light bursting, and on top of that, the sound of another body falling. From the gray route entrance to the inner wall, in a space of perhaps thirty human paces, so many bodies were moving at once that no one could follow them all.

One was already lying on the floor, the body that had taken three in front of the child earlier and gone down with them. Each time Serakion units stepped over it, a face with one eye line still half lit shook under their feet.

Only a few things reached Sion's eyes.

A smiling mouth.
A crooked mouth.
A face drawn round.

He held his blade, but there was nowhere to use it. It was all too fast; before he could take a step, the positions had changed. He stood between the rear line and the front line and watched from there.

The expressions that only a moment ago had come into being like a game at the tips of children's fingers were now shaking amid the dust and shards.

The androids that had just been given faces were still strong.

One put three of the Serakion legion down almost at once. Before the hand snapping the first unit's neck had even stopped, its foot had smashed the second unit's knee, and the third unit it threw on top of the other two. The thrown body hit the wall and bounced twice.

Another had half its body smashed. Its chest plate was folded inward, and the light inside was leaking out. With that body it caught an enemy unit trying to get at the children and wrenched it the other way and hurled it, all the way.

The arm it threw with did not come back. It came off at the shoulder and stayed on the floor. With its one remaining arm, the android caught the next unit by the shoulder.

Luhai looked at the arm on the floor. Its fingers were still clenched.

"That."

He said it.

No one took it up.

The Serakion side did not hesitate. When the front rank broke, the next rank stepped straight into its place, and when the next rank was stopped, it rammed in bodily to force a way. Even as each body stepped over the broken ones, the spacing of their steps did not slip once.

The androids on this side fought separately, each one, to the end. They moved in the same direction, but they didn't look like the same being.

One tried to cover two children at once and its right side gave out first,
one chose to wrap up as many enemy units as it could and drop away with them,
one, on legs nearly wrecked, never stepped back.

On the rear line, Seorin was pressing people against the wall. She took one person by the shoulder and turned them, pushed the next one's back, and one Hazran man who wouldn't move she pulled by the arm and stood against the wall. When the two old machines shoved the blocked gap wider with their shoulders from inside the rear line, people slipped out through it one at a time.

"Hurry."

Seorin said.

"Don't look. Go."

The old Hazran man, passing with his plate held to his chest, stopped in front of the gap and looked at the battle. He went in only after Seorin pushed him in the back.

Han Jiwoo kept pushing the children back, but her eyes kept going to the front line. Her pushing hands did not stop, but her head kept turning the other way.

"Get back!"

She shouted again.

"Don't look back!"

Only the children's bodies were dragged away. Their eyes stayed on the androids.

When Aka pushed two children into the gap, one of them turned back in it.

"Mine's still standing."

"It's standing. Go."

Aka said.

Even after the child had gone, Aka turned in the gap and watched the front line for a long time.

The second android to go dark was the unit that had been given a frowning mouth. The very face a child had drawn, laughing, and said, "Because this one's kind of grumpy anyway."

Serakion units were crowding into the side passage. It was a way that came back around toward the children. That android reached the mouth first, and instead of going in, it turned around and drove both arms into the walls on either side of the mouth.

Like a door.

The first unit hit its chest.
The second unit hit.
The third unit burst into light the moment it hit.

The wall rang once, and the android's body was half torn apart. The arms did not come out of the walls. The mouth stayed blocked, and nothing came over from behind it.

A child saw it and screamed.

"That's mine…!"

The cry leapt up over the sound of metal.

The child tried to bolt from the rear line, but Luhai caught the child's wrist first. Even when the child bit that hand, Luhai did not let go. Without letting go, he could not say a word. The child's teeth sank into the back of his hand and drew blood, and still Luhai did not pull his hand away.

"Bite."

He said it.

"Keep biting. I'm not letting you go."

Only when a Hazran woman picked the child up from behind did Luhai let go. In her arms, the child reached toward the android standing at the mouth of the passage. Soil was still dried on the fingertips. They were the fingers that had drawn that mouth earlier.

One of the old machines lowered itself for the first time. Its knee joint stopped half folded; the joint was old, and it made a sound as it folded. Its optical eye did not leave the battle. The other old machine beside it laid a hand on its shoulder, but the lowered body did not rise.

"Get up."

The machine beside it spoke.

"The gap is narrowing."

The lowered machine did not rise. Its optical eye was on the battle. The machine beside it went to the gap alone and pushed with its shoulder.

Sern was still holding the record panel. His hand had stopped over it. Numbers were written on the record panel only as far as the third line, and after that it was blank.

Ater was pressed to the edge of the upper route, looking down. The finger counting the ranks coming in from beyond the gray route stopped at four, then moved again.

He spoke low, through clenched teeth.

"The self-destruct ranks are growing."

Only then did Sern look up.

"From the fourth rank on, the spacing has been cut in half."

"Half means what."

Seorin asked.

"The front rank cannot take them. The next ones arrive before it can."

"How long can they hold."

"I do not know. I am not counting. I am watching."

Sern answered.

Seorin struck the next person's shoulder.

Sion saw it too. The units coming in from the back did not slow. They were already leaning forward before they reached the front rank, and wherever they were blocked they rammed straight in. Each time light burst where they rammed, the bodies on this side that were holding were pushed back a hand's width.

A sound slipped out of Han Jiwoo's mouth.

"Stop…"

As her grip tightened on a child's shoulder, the child looked up at her. She did not look at the child, and the strength in her hand did not ease.

"Who are you saying that to."

Luhai asked. Blood ran down the back of his hand.

Han Jiwoo did not answer. That side or this side. Neither one stopped.

Nothing stopped.

The third android to fall was the one with the big round eyes a child had drawn.

To the last, that unit held its pose in front of the children, both arms spread.

An enemy unit rammed its chest.
The arms did not close.
A second unit pierced its side.
The arms did not close.

Even after its torso was nearly half torn open, those arms stayed spread as wide as the ones behind it.

Just before it fell, its optical eye blinked, very briefly. The optical eye that had not left the enemy unit ramming its chest went out after that.

As the spread arms touched the floor, the light of the round eye lines came on one last time and went out.

The children's sounds changed. The screams grew shorter, and crying filled the spaces between. One child's breath caught while crying and the sound broke off, and a Hazran woman stroked the child's back. Another child did not cry. The child's mouth hung open, and no sound came out.

The sound climbed the walls all the way to the upper level, but nothing came down from the dark up there.

From above, Ater looked into that dark.

"They are not coming."

Ater said.

"No more are coming. The fourth rank is the last."

"The last?"

"For now."

Sern wrote those words below the fourth line.

And in the middle of it all, one of those still standing looked back for just a moment.

At the end of that gaze were the crying children, Han Jiwoo holding them close, and the people frozen as they watched all of it.

Han Jiwoo, still holding the child, met that gaze.

Then the android turned forward again.

Two lay on the floor ahead, and at the mouth of the side passage one had gone dark standing. He set his foot down between the two on the floor. He did not step on either body. The Serakion units had stepped on them. He did not.

One step.
A pause.
One step.
A pause.

Han Jiwoo watched his feet. The child watched too. The child's crying stopped.

One beat.

It started again.
