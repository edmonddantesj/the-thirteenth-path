# Episode 177. Before the Word Soul

While the last android held on alone, every eye in the old layer was on him.

The Serakion legion kept pushing in, and he had already been whittling his own body away again and again to stop that wave.

The people on the rear line no longer counted. At first they had counted the enemy units that fell, then the parts that dropped from his body. Now they counted nothing and only watched.

Only Sern was still counting.

"The fifth line."

Seorin answered.

"Spacing?"

"In twos. It is still the same."

Seorin turned her head toward Han Jiwoo.

"Jiwoo. Kids farther back."

Han Jiwoo looked behind her. It was the wall. The children's backs were against it.

"This is the back."

Seorin looked at that wall and, saying nothing more, looked toward the rear gap. Past the gap was a passage, but what lay past the passage, no one could tell. She did not send the children that way.

Once again the last android crossed two Serakion units into each other. He caught the first unit's arm and shoved it toward the second unit, and while the two collided he broke the central axis of the third unit coming in behind them. Three motions ran together into one. Each motion was shorter than when he had first fought.

He pushed the three he had broken to the edge of the passage. What was piled there now came up to his waist, so the heap was blocking one of the side lines. The Serakion units did not climb over the heap; they came only from the front.

Luhai looked at the heap, then moved his eyes to the piece of wall panel he was holding. When he stood the wall panel up beside the heap, the heap rose by a hand's width. As he drew back to the rear line again, he looked once at the android's back.

A child was looking at that back. The Hazran woman tried to turn the child's shoulders away, but the child pulled free and turned back, and the woman did not turn the child again. The child's eyes were on the android's flank. Light was leaking from the place that had been scraped earlier.

That child barely swallowed a sob and spoke in a very small voice.

"He… must be hurting."

The words were so small that only the few people close by heard them.

Han Jiwoo lifted her head. Sion heard it too. One of the old machines took its hand off the wall.

The Hazran woman looked down at the child, but the child was looking at the android. The woman said nothing, yet her hand on the child's shoulder tightened.

Almost in the same moment, the last android's movement wavered, very briefly.

He was a beat late taking his hand off the third unit he had just cut down. Through that opening, a Serakion unit's arm raked deep across his flank. Metal tore and light rippled, and his body was pushed back.

Sion saw only that the waver had come at the same time as the child's words.

Han Jiwoo's eyes went wide, and the hand on the back of the child she held went still. Her mouth opened a little, then closed. No sound came out.

Seorin looked at Han Jiwoo.

"What."

Han Jiwoo shook her head once, and Seorin turned her eyes toward the android.

The last android straightened again. Light was leaking from the raked flank. He pressed a hand over the light once. The leaking did not stop. When he took the hand away, there was light on it. He looked at that hand.

One beat.

Then he took the next unit with that hand.

And very briefly, truly very briefly, he looked behind him.

At the end of that look was the child who had said those words.

A face worn out from crying, eyes that were terrified and still kept looking his way. Dirt-stained fingers clutching the hem of the child's own clothes.

The child looked at him, too. Their eyes held for as long as it takes to lift a foot once.

The child took hold of the Hazran woman's hand. The woman looked down, but the child was looking at the android.

The Serakion legion did not miss the opening. Two units pushed in at once.

Sern spoke.

"Two, front."

The last android's response was different.

Instead of cutting them down at once as before, he first turned his body farther inward, covering the direction of the children behind him with his torso. The front narrowed by as much as he had turned. He took the two on that narrowed front.

The collision rang out loud. What was left of his shoulder axis twisted deeper, and one optical eye could not come back on for a while. A crack opened in his chest plate and climbed to just below the mouth line the child had drawn for him.

The children swallowed their screams.

He did not give a single step.

Of the two he had taken, he broke one's neck with his remaining arm and drove the other against the wall with his wrenched shoulder. When that unit tried to move again, he pressed it down with his own torso and did not pull away until its light went out. Once he had, he shoved the unit under the bottom of the heap at the edge so the heap would not collapse.

When Han Jiwoo loosened the arm holding the child, the freed hand stopped in front of her chest. It was a hand reaching to take hold of something, but there was nothing to take hold of, so she brought it back to the child's back.

Her lips moved once, but no words came. They moved once more. Again nothing came. She bit her lip.

Sion saw her lips. He had been watching the people, as Seorin had told him to, and had caught her lips while doing it. Han Jiwoo's eyes were on the android, and Sion turned his own eyes back to the people.

One of the old machines muttered, very low.

"…He hears."

Who it was meant for, no one asked.

The machine standing beside that one turned its head toward the child, then toward the android, and did not turn its head back.

From the high side, Ater looked down at Sern.

"Are you recording this?"

Sern's eyes moved from the record panel to the child, and from the child to the android.

"I am."

He wrote one line. After he wrote it, he held the record panel against his chest.

The machine that had turned its head came away from the wall, walked slowly to the end of the heap at the edge of the passage, and stood there. The gap left between the heap and the wall was the width of that machine's body. It put its body into the gap. The side line was sealed completely.

Luhai watched that, then looked at the wall panel he had stood up. He said nothing.

The child did not say the words again, but the hand gripping Han Jiwoo's clothes tightened. The Hazran woman laid her hand on the child's head.

The child looked up at Han Jiwoo.

"What do you do if he's hurting?"

Han Jiwoo didn't answer right away; she looked at the android's flank, at the light leaking from it, and then at her own hands.

"…You fix it."

The child looked at those hands.

"Can you fix it?"

"Won't know till I try."

When the child looked at the android again, the hand gripping Han Jiwoo's clothes loosened a little.

The Hazran woman looked at Han Jiwoo.

"Can it be fixed?"

Han Jiwoo didn't look at the woman.

"Machines can be fixed."

The woman asked nothing more, but she did not take her hand off the child's head either. Han Jiwoo covered that hand with her own. With it covered, she watched the battle.

Sion saw the two of them, and he had seen Han Jiwoo look at her own hands, too. When he turned his eyes back to the people, the man in front of the gap was still there. The hand that had been scraping the wall had gone still.

The last android moved again.

This time it was rougher. He didn't wait for the unit coming into the mouth of the route; he stepped out and took it. While he pressed the unit he had taken to the floor and broke it, the light leaking from his flank dripped onto the floor. The light on the floor stayed for a beat, then vanished. He stood where he was and waited for the next unit.

Sion looked at where he stood. It was one step ahead of where he had stood when he looked back earlier. The space between him and the children had widened by one step, and the space between him and the enemy had narrowed by that same step.

Seorin saw that one step too.

"Sern."

"Yes."

"How many are left?"

Sern looked at the mouth of the route, but beyond it was dark.

"Two lines are visible. Behind them, I do not know."

Seorin nodded. The hand at her waist closed on the hilt. She did not draw.

While the next line of the Serakion legion took its place at the mouth, light leaked from the crack in the last android's chest plate. Above that light, the short mouth line was lit.

The child was watching that mouth line. Han Jiwoo was watching it too. Both of them were watching to see if it would go out.

It did not.
