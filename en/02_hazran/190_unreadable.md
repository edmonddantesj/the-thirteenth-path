# Episode 190. Unreadable

The guide lights were polite.
A lane that should have opened opened half a beat late.
A barrier panel that should have closed closed early.
Too early.
It tripped its own legion.
One unit caught its foot on the barrier panel's edge and pitched forward.
The one behind ran into its back.
A recovery line came.
By the shortest path.
It filled the empty spot.
A spot already emptied.
One more time.

Seorin kept her eyes on that flow.

"Don't just look ahead."

She kept her voice low.

"Watch where the recovery line trips over its own feet."

Sion didn't look at the unit in front of him.
He looked behind it.
Where it got filled when one fell.
Which row came over when a row went empty.
He touched the floor with a finger.
Here.
Here.
The third was filled before he could touch it.

"The third one's fast."

He said.

"The third is always fast."

Seorin said.

"So don't watch the third. Watch the fourth."

"Where's the fourth?"

"Next to where the third filled."

Seorin said.

"If the third is fast, the fourth is late. Always."

Sion looked beside the spot the third had filled.
It was empty.
Still.
He put his finger there.

The old machines knew that beat.
They were slow.
Not a young speed.
One old machine drew back half a step late, on purpose.
Two Serakion units came in at the same moment to fill that empty square.
In that instant another machine shoved one unit's axis from the flank.
The shoved unit slid along the gray wall, scraping its shoulder.
The unit following close behind caught its foot in that spot.
It staggered.
Once.
The row fell out of line.
Serakion tried to make the straightest row.
So the moment that row went off, the whole of it tripped on its own feet.

Luhai drove the iron bar into the staggering unit's knee.
The iron bar bent.
The unit buckled.
Luhai looked at the bent iron bar.

"How many more can this take?"

"Two."

Kael said from the left axis.

"On the third, your wrist goes."

Luhai shifted his grip on the iron bar.
Bent side out.

The old machine looked at the iron bar.
It looked at its own arm.

"Use my arm."

It said.

"It does not bend."

"Your arm's yours to use."

Luhai said.

"Two more, he said."

The old machine lowered its arm.
It stood beside Luhai.
Half a step ahead of him.

Han Jiwoo was on the same axis as the last android.
At the drawn-back distance.
Beside the wall panel.
The two Hazran men's arms shook.
She watched what he looked at first.
What he reacted to first.
She spoke short.

"Not the front. Behind."

He looked behind.

"Third row now."

He stood in front of the third row.

"Stop there and the right opens."

He stopped.
The right opened.
Luhai and the old machine went in there.

The gaps between her words grew shorter.
The gaps between his moves too.
Sion saw it.
Between Han Jiwoo's mouth and the android's feet.
There was no gap.

The last android was on the edge.
His shoulder axis made a sound.
Every time he came back.
One optical eye lost its light.
A flicker.
It came back.
A flicker.
He didn't touch his optical eye with his hand.
Han Jiwoo counted the flickers.
Four.
Five.

"Are you okay?"

She asked.

He didn't answer.
The optical eye came back.
He looked at her.
Once.
He looked ahead.

Han Jiwoo passed the wall panel to a Hazran man.
Her hands were empty.
She put a hand on his shoulder.
Over the knot.
She pressed.
The shoulder came up half a knuckle.
She took her hand away.
The shoulder sank back down.

"I can't fix it now."

She said.

"When it's over."

He was looking ahead.

Seorin drew a breath.

"Open the inside from here."

Her voice was low.

"Before the center reads us,
we reach it first."

"Luhai."

Luhai looked back.

"Go down."

"Now?"

"Now. Don't leave the lower level empty. Take those two."

Seorin pointed her chin at the two Hazran men.
The wall panel.

"We're short of hands here."

Luhai said.

"Down there is shorter."

Seorin said.

"The wall panel won't work inside. There isn't even room for three people to stand."

Luhai looked at the wall panel.
He looked at the inner corridor.
It was narrow.
He looked at his bent iron bar.

"Got it."

He said.

The two Hazran men set the wall panel down.
They let their arms hang.
Luhai looked at Han Jiwoo.
He looked at the last android.

"His shoulder."

"I know."

Han Jiwoo said.

"Go."

Luhai went.
The two Hazran men behind him.
The wall panel left behind.
Han Jiwoo looked at the wall panel.
It was still standing where it had been set.

Luhai looked back from the corner.

"Jiwoo."

Han Jiwoo looked at him.

"If his shoulder goes, you go too. Come back together, the two of you."

"Okay."

"That's a promise."

"I said okay."

Luhai turned the corner.
His footsteps faded.
Human footsteps.
Uneven.
Serakion's floor didn't give the sound back.

Each axis moved.

On the left, Kael cut a recovery line.
One panel.
The cut recovery line pushed into the upper drop line.
The pushed drop line slowed the central circulation line.
A drop-plate overhead came halfway down and stopped.
It trembled.
It went neither down nor up.

Sern looked at the record panel.

"Realignment is late. One beat."

"And one beat means?"

"One person gets through. Under the drop-plate."

"If the plate falls?"

"If it falls, that person is crushed."

Sern said.

Sion looked at the plate.
The plate halfway down.
It was trembling.
He drew a breath.
He didn't let it out.

Sion went in on that one beat.
Under the half-stopped drop-plate.
He dropped low.
The plate trembled above his back.
He was through.
The inner bulkhead.
The seam at its side.
He set the tip of the blade to it.
He pressed.

The bulkhead opened.

Quietly.
No tearing sound.
No breaking sound either.
The wall folded.
Like a path that was meant to open.
The guidance tone came.
"The route has been rearranged."
The path that must never open
it announced as an open path.
In the same voice.

What lay beyond came into view.

Deeper.
Narrower.
The walls smoother.
The light lower.
Three rows of boundary lights on the floor.
Running far.
No end in sight.

Sion swallowed.
The hand on the blade dropped.
It came back up.

Sern spoke very low.

"Core approach line, opening."

"Have they read us?"

Seorin asked.

Sern looked at the record panel.
He listened to the guidance tone.
He looked at the boundary lights across the corridor.
Their color didn't change.

"Not yet."

He said.

"They were the side that read first. Now we are the ones who are in first."

Seorin didn't nod.
She looked at the open bulkhead.
Past it.

"Once we're in, we can't get out."

She said.

To no one in particular.

"I know."

Sion said.

"And down below?"

Han Jiwoo asked.

"Luhai's there."

Seorin said.

"The first and second recovery hands too. The wall-side machine too."

"The children?"

"Aka's there."

Han Jiwoo looked at the wall panel standing there.
She left it there.

Ater came down from above.
Dust on his shoulder.
He didn't brush it off.
Sern tucked the record panel under his arm.
Kael came over from the left axis.
A fragment of panel in his hand.
He tossed it.
The two old machines stood behind.
The last android stood beside Han Jiwoo.
His shoulder axis made a sound.
Han Jiwoo heard it.

"The front isn't yours."

She said.

"Behind. After they're shaken."

He turned his head.
He looked at her.
He looked ahead again.

Seorin crossed the bulkhead first.
Sion.
Sern.
Ater.
Kael.
The two old machines.
Han Jiwoo.
The last android last.
His shoulder touched the edge of the bulkhead.
He didn't stop.

Behind them the bulkhead closed.
Quietly.
The guidance tone came once more.
It announced the closed path as closed.
This time it was right.

The inner corridor.
Three rows of boundary lights.
Nothing came.
Sern opened the record panel again.
He wrote nothing.
There was nothing to write.

"It's not reading us?"

Sion asked.

"It is reading."

Sern said.

"It has not yet decided what we are."

"And when it decides?"

"When it decides, they will come."

Sern said.

"Not now. It does not block what it does not know. It counts first, then blocks."

"How long will that take?"

"I do not know. We are the first to come in here."

Kael rapped on the corridor wall with a knuckle.
It made no sound.
Thick.
The same as the walls on the lower level.

"The walls are the same."

He said.

"The walls are the same."

Sern said.

"What does the reading is different."

Sion looked at the end of the corridor.
There was no end.
Three rows of light.
His own shadow wasn't on them.
The light was coming from below.
He lifted his foot.
He set it down.
The boundary light didn't change color under his foot.
Not yet.

The last android stood beside him.
He lifted his foot.
He set it down.
The boundary light changed color under him.
Once.
It went back.

"You, it reads."

Sion said.

Sern wrote on the record panel.
One line.

"That android is the only one they know."

He said.

"Us, they do not know yet."

Han Jiwoo looked under his foot.
The color that had gone back.
She set her own foot beside it.
It didn't change.
Their two feet side by side.
One read, one not.
